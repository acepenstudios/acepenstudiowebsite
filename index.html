What we do

We work with writers to get their stories published.

Writing is only one part of the work. We help you develop your story, shape the manuscript, position you as an author, and navigate the path toward publication.

About the studio

We are a boutique literary agency and creative studio built around African writers, ideas and stories.

We believe good writing deserves serious attention — but serious attention does not always mean the same thing.

One project may need representation. Another may need a better manuscript. Another may need a writing partner, an editor, a publishing strategy or someone who understands what to do with a publishing opportunity that has landed in its inbox.

We look at the work first, then work out what it needs.

Literary Representation

Editorial Development

05

You don't know what publishing is all about? Don't sweat it. We give you practical guidance when you need to understand an offer, assess a route to publication, prepare a book proposal for a publishing house or simply just make a better next move.

How we work

Not every project needs an agent.

Sometimes the right next step is representation. Sometimes it is editorial work, manuscript development, writing support or simply better information.

We do not treat every inquiry as a representation opportunity. We assess the project in front of us and recommend the most useful next step.

That also means you do not need to arrive knowing exactly which service you need. You do need to give us enough context to understand what you are building.

Who we work with

Writers

First-time authors, experienced writers, self-published authors and people somewhere in between.

People with stories

Founders, professionals, public figures, organisations and other people whose expertise or experience needs a strong written form.

Creative partners

Publishers, institutions, brands and organisations looking for thoughtful literary, editorial or storytelling collaboration.

Projects with potential

We care less about whether a project arrives perfectly packaged and more about whether there is something worth building.
