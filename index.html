What we do

We help writers turn ideas and manuscripts into books with a future.

Writing the book is only part of the journey. We work with writers at different stages of the process — from developing an early idea to shaping a manuscript, defining an author's position and pursuing publishing and commercial opportunities.

For writers

From first idea to the wider world.

Literary Representation

We represent selected authors and help identify publishing, media, partnership and commercial opportunities for their work.

Editorial Development

We work with writers to strengthen manuscripts, sharpen their ideas and shape stories that are compelling, coherent and ready for their intended audience.

Ghostwriting

Through our ghostwriting imprint, MS – Your Writing Consultant, we help individuals turn their ideas, experiences and expertise into fully developed books.

Author Positioning

We help authors understand what makes their work distinctive and develop a stronger identity around their writing, expertise and story.

Publishing Strategy

We help writers think beyond the manuscript — considering publishing routes, audiences, partnerships, rights and the opportunities that can grow around a book.

Workshops & Development

We create spaces for writers to develop their craft, understand the publishing landscape and connect with other people working in the literary world.

Why Ace Pen Studio

Because a good book needs more than good writing.

Editorial thinking

We look closely at the story itself — its voice, structure, argument, characters and potential.

Strategic thinking

We think about where a book belongs, who it is for and what opportunities can be built around it.

Author-first relationships

We believe the relationship between an author and their representative should be thoughtful, transparent and long-term.
