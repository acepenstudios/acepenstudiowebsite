A note about publishing

Publishing works differently in different parts of the world. Since Ace Pen Studio works primarily with writers and publishers in Tanzania, East Africa and the wider African publishing market, some of the things you may have heard about publishing elsewhere may not apply here.

In some markets, authors may receive an advance from a publisher when their book is accepted. This is not always the case in Tanzania. A publisher may instead agree to publish a manuscript without paying an advance, with the author earning royalties from sales later.

Having a literary agent also does not automatically mean that you will receive an advance, a publishing deal, or immediate income.

This is why we look at each writer and project individually. Sometimes the best way for us to support you may be through literary representation. In other cases, your project may first need writing, editing, manuscript development, publishing advice or other support before it is ready to be presented to publishers.

If we think you would benefit from one of these services, we will explain why, what we recommend, and what it will cost before any work begins.

Don't worry, you are not expected to know which service you need when you first contact us. That's part of what we help you figure out.
