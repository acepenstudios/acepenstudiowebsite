About Ace Pen

Ace Pen Studio is a literary agency and creative writing studio built around one simple belief: good stories deserve serious attention.

We work with writers and project owners at different stages of the creative process — from an idea that needs shaping, to a manuscript that needs development, to a publishing opportunity that needs professional guidance.

Our work sits between creativity and strategy. We care about the writing itself, but we also care about what happens around it: the author, the audience, the market, the opportunity and the path forward.

01 / Services

Help us find you the right support for the work in front of you.

Not every project needs the same thing. Sometimes the next step is representation. Sometimes it is better writing, a stronger manuscript or a clearer publishing strategy.

01

Literary Representation

For selected writers and projects, we provide representation and help navigate publisher relationships, submissions, opportunities and negotiations.

02

Editorial & Manuscript Development

We help writers turn promising material into stronger, clearer and more purposeful work through developmental editing, narrative development, structural thinking and manuscript support.

03

Ghostwriting & Writing Support

Through MS – Your Writing Consultant, our confidential writing practice, we help people turn ideas, expertise and lived experience into books you can be proud of. Your voice, our pen, your story!

04

Author Development & Positioning

We help authors think beyond the manuscript by finding you the right literary opportunities, author positioning, media kits, and long-term profile development.

05

Publishing Strategy & Consultancy

We provide practical guidance on publishing routes, opportunities, proposals, positioning and the decisions that surround taking a project to market.

06

Workshops, Bootcamps & Partnerships

02 / Process

We start with the project, not the package.

We do not assume every writer needs an agent, an editor or a ghostwriter. We first understand what you are actually trying to build, then recommend the most useful path.

01

Start with the project

Tell us what you are working on, where it stands and what has brought you to us.

02

Understand what it needs

We look at the project, the stage it is in and the outcome you are trying to achieve.

03

Recommend a path

We identify the kind of support that makes sense rather than forcing every project into the same service.

04

Agree scope & terms

Before paid work begins, we explain what we recommend, what it involves and what it will cost.

05

Do the work

Once we agree to work together, we get on with the actual work of making the project stronger.

04 / A Note

Representation is not the only way we can help.

And having an idea does not automatically mean you need an agent.

We would rather recommend the right thing than sell you the wrong thing.

A writer may need representation. Another may need to finish the manuscript first. Someone else may need a ghostwriter, editorial development, publishing advice or help positioning an existing body of work.

We assess projects individually. Where representation makes sense, we can discuss it. Where another form of support is more useful, we will say so.

General publishing questions do not require a private consultation. We are building our FAQ to answer the questions we receive most often, so start there if you are looking for general information.
