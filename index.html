About Ace Pen

We believe African writers deserve professional support built around the realities of African publishing.

We want to help build a stronger ecosystem around African writers and their work. That means taking writing seriously as both a creative practice and a professional endeavour.

Some writers come to us with a book they have already written. Others simply have an idea they can't get out of their heads, or years of experience they cannot wait to tell, or a story they know needs to be written but cannot yet shape into a book.

Our role is not to assume that every writer needs the same thing. We look at the work, the writer, the goal and the publishing context, then determine what kind of support makes sense.

02

What We Do

Literary Representation

We represent selected writers and their books, helping develop publishing opportunities and navigate relationships with publishers and other industry partners.

Manuscript Development & Editing

We help writers tell their stories by strengthening the structure, argument, narrative, voice, clarity and overall readiness for publication. Sometimes the work needs editing. Sometimes it needs deeper development before it is ready to be edited.

Writing & Ghostwriting

Through MS — Your Writing Consultant, we provide confidential writing and ghostwriting support for people who have a story, but maybe not the time or skills to turn it into a book. We help you turn your expertise and body of work into a book you can be proud of. Our pen, your voice!

Publishing Consultancy

We help writers understand their publishing options, assess opportunities and prepare strategically for conversations with publishers and other publishing professionals.

Author Development & Positioning

A book does not exist in isolation from its author. We support selected writers with author positioning, media materials, public-facing storytelling and other tools that help build a sustainable author profile.

Workshops & Literary Programmes

We develop workshops, bootcamps, conversations and other programmes designed to create practical pathways for writers and strengthen literary communities.

03

Representation

Literary representation is not simply about finding a publisher. It is about understanding where a particular work belongs, what it needs before it gets there, and how to build a professional relationship around it.

We do not believe every project should be represented immediately.

A manuscript may need development first. An idea may need to be written. A writer may need help understanding their options. A publishing opportunity may need to be assessed before anyone signs anything.

That is why our first step is understanding the project rather than promising a publishing deal.

Who We Work With

First-time writers

You do not need a publishing history to have a serious project. If you have a strong idea and a genuine commitment to developing it, there may be a place for us to start.

Established writers

We also work with writers who have already published and are looking for editorial, strategic, publishing or representation support for their next stage.

Experts with stories to tell

Researchers, professionals, entrepreneurs, academics and public figures often have valuable knowledge that has never been shaped into a book. We can help explore that possibility.

People with a story

Memoir, biography, lived experience and personal histories can become meaningful books when the story is approached with care, structure and editorial discipline.

05

Publishing in Context

Publishing does not work the same way everywhere. The realities of publishing in Tanzania and across Africa are different from the publishing models writers may encounter in North America, the UK or other international markets.

An agent does not automatically mean a publishing deal. A publishing deal does not automatically mean an advance. And a completed manuscript does not automatically mean that it is ready to be submitted.

We therefore approach each project individually. Where representation makes sense, we can explore it. Where the work needs writing, editing, development or strategic support first, we will say so.

Our aim is to give writers a clearer understanding of where they are, what their work needs, and what a realistic next step looks like.
