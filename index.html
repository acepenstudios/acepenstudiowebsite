Before you leave, here's a note about publishing

Publishing does not look exactly the same everywhere. In Tanzania and across Africa, publishing structures, contracts, advances, distribution and author opportunities can differ significantly from the models you may have seen elsewhere.

An advance is not guaranteed, and it is not necessarily part of every publishing arrangement. Working with a literary representative also does not guarantee an advance, a publishing deal, or future income.

What we can do is help you understand your options, develop the work, position it appropriately and determine what kind of opportunity makes sense for you. If we recommend a paid service, the scope and fees will always be explained before any work begins.
