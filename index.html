A Note About Publishing

Publishing does not work exactly the same way in every market. Tanzania, East Africa and the wider African publishing ecosystem have their own structures, opportunities and limitations.

Advances are not always a standard part of publishing agreements in our market, and working with a literary agent does not automatically mean that you will receive an advance, secure a publishing deal or earn immediate income from your work.

We look at each writer and project individually. Depending on what you tell us, we may recommend literary representation, writing, editing, manuscript development, publishing advice or another form of support.

If we recommend a particular service or approach, we will explain why we think it is appropriate and what it would involve. Any costs, services or representation arrangements will be discussed with you before work begins.

You do not need to know which service you need before contacting us.
