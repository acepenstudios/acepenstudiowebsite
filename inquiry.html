A note about publishing

Publishing works differently in different markets. Since we work primarily across Tanzania, East Africa and the wider African publishing landscape, some of the things you may have heard about publishing elsewhere may not apply here.

For example, a publishing deal does not automatically mean an advance. An agent does not automatically mean a deal or immediate income either. Sometimes the best next step is representation; sometimes it is writing, editing, manuscript development or publishing advice first.

We'll look at the project and tell you what we think makes sense. If we recommend paid work, we'll explain the scope and cost before anything begins.

06 · Before you submit

You do not need to send your manuscript with this form. We want the context first.

If we need to see your work, we'll tell you what would be useful.

And one last thing: this is an inquiry, not an automatic representation agreement. If we think there is a fit, we'll have a separate conversation about what happens next.
