A note about publishing

Publishing works differently in different markets. Since we work primarily across Tanzania, East Africa and the wider African publishing landscape, some of the things you may have heard about publishing elsewhere may not apply here.

A publishing deal does not automatically mean an advance. An agent does not automatically mean a deal or immediate income either. Sometimes the best next step is representation; sometimes it is writing, editing, manuscript development or publishing advice first.

We'll look at the project and tell you what we think makes sense. If we recommend paid work, we'll explain the scope and cost before anything begins.

06 · Before you submit

A few things to know.

What happens after you send your inquiry.

You do not need to send your manuscript with this form. We want the context first.

Once we have reviewed your inquiry, we'll consider whether the project is a fit for the studio and what a useful next step might look like. That may involve a paid consultation, a defined service or a separate project engagement.

An inquiry does not automatically create an appointment or consultation. Any paid work, fees, scope or representation arrangement will be discussed and agreed separately before work begins.

About your information We collect the information you provide so Ace Pen Studio can understand your project, assess whether it falls within the studio's work, respond to your inquiry and determine an appropriate next step. We use this information for purposes connected to your inquiry and our professional communication with you. Please do not include confidential material or sensitive personal information that is not necessary for us to understand the project.
